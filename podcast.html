Brothers in Music:
The A. R. Rahman Edition

My brother, M V Swaroop — a trained Indian classical flautist (and lawyer) — and I co-host a podcast on music we love. We start with methodically exploring the rich discography of Oscar- and Grammy-winning Indian film composer A R Rahman, year by year, from 1992 to 2024.
